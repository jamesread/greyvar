# Shop signs

Icons for the hanging signs over shop doors, generated with PixelLab
`create_image_pixen` (16x16, transparent, black outline):

| file        | sign          | seed | prompt                                                   |
|-------------|---------------|------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| hair.png    | signHair      | 800  | hanging wooden sign with scissors and a comb             |
| tailor.png  | signTailor    | 811  | hanging sign with one big bright red spool of thread     |
| science.png | signScience   | 802  | hanging wooden sign with a round green potion flask      |
| farm.png    | signFarm      | 803  | hanging wooden sign with a golden sheaf of wheat         |

They are packed in this order into `res/img/textures/entities/shopsigns.png`
(used by `server/dat/entdefs/shopsigns.tsx`).
